Xiaoan Translate · Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · App: Xiaoan Translate (小安翻译), Android package com.jbk.xiaoants

Xiaoan Translate identifies languages and translates text on your device. History and favorites stay on your device. Language models require a network connection to download, and Google ML Kit collects certain device, app and usage diagnostics. This policy explains where data goes and how to delete it.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Xiaoan Translate (小安翻译), provided by developer Baokang Jia (贾保康). The app does not require registration or sign-in and does not create user accounts. We do not operate a server that receives your source text, translations, history or favorites. We do not use that content for advertising or sell user data.

2. Data the app processes

  • Translation content: Text you type or paste, the detected or selected languages, and translations are processed on your device. Google ML Kit's language identification and translation APIs run on-device; those APIs do not send the source text or translations to Google's servers. The app does not upload them to a server operated by us.
  • Local records and preferences: After a successful translation, the app saves the source text, translation, languages, time, favorite status and translation engine in a local database. Theme, default target language and interface language preferences are also stored locally. History search runs on your device.
  • Language models and network: If a required language model has not been installed, the app downloads it through Google ML Kit. Model or SDK updates may also contact Google services. Downloads require Wi-Fi by default; after a failed download, you may explicitly allow mobile data. You can view or delete downloaded models in the app.
  • Diagnostics and usage: The Google ML Kit SDK may send Google device model, operating system version, available accelerators, app package name and version, installation identifiers, performance metrics, API events, error codes, input and output sizes, identified languages, and configured source and target languages. Google uses these data for diagnostics, usage measurement, maintenance and improvement. Google states that these data are transmitted over HTTPS. The exact data depend on the ML Kit features and SDK version in use.

3. Third parties and sharing you initiate

  • Google: The app uses Google ML Kit for language identification, on-device translation and model management. Google handles the model requests and SDK diagnostics described above; ML Kit processes translation content on your device. See the ML Kit Terms and Privacy, ML Kit data disclosure guide and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Device speech service: When you choose to hear text read aloud, the app passes it to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The engine may be supplied by your device maker or another provider. Whether it uses a network connection and how it handles the text depend on that engine and its privacy policy.
  • Clipboard and apps you choose: When you choose Copy or Share, the relevant text is sent to the system clipboard or share sheet. A receiving app gets shared text only when you select it, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Apart from these SDK activities and features you initiate, the app does not send translation content to other third parties. It does not include an advertising SDK or a separate behavioral analytics service. If you email us, we receive the address and message you provide in order to respond.

4. Retention, control and deletion

Local translation records, favorites and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can delete individual records in History or clear all translation history in Settings; clearing history also removes favorites. You can delete downloaded language models under Settings → Offline language packs. Android system backup is disabled for the app.

Uninstalling the app causes the operating system to remove its local app data. Because we do not operate user accounts or a translation data server, we cannot remotely view or delete records on your device. Data held by Google, your device's speech engine or an app you choose to share with is governed by their respective policies and deletion controls.

5. Security and permissions

Translation history is kept in the Android app's private storage, which is protected by the device operating system. According to Google, ML Kit transmits its network data over HTTPS. The app uses internet access to download language models and access SDK services. The current version does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or broad device-file access permissions. Avoid using an untrusted speech engine or sharing app for text you need to keep confidential.

6. Children

Xiaoan Translate is not specifically directed at children. It does not create accounts or serve targeted advertising. If you have questions about a child's use of the app, please contact us below.

7. Updates to this policy

If the app's features, SDKs or data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Where applicable, we will notify you of material changes in the app.
